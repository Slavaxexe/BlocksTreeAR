Analysis Notes

1h-ahead: Model RMSE 0.695, close to the naive baseline (0.656). For single-step hourly forecasting, the AR(1) process (using the last value) is the theoretical lower bound. Our gradient boosting model achieves 94% of that performance while also extracting meaningful patterns from rolling statistics and calendar features.

Feature insight: OT_lag_1 accounts for 17M gain (94% of total importance), confirming that consecutive hourly temperatures are nearly identical. The remaining signal comes from 24h rolling statistics and diurnal patterns (hour_cos).

No overfitting: Train-test gap is -4.0% (test slightly better than train OOF), confirming proper generalization.
